# Procus collaboration notes

## Design is not locked

The founder explicitly clarified on 19 September 2026 that the current codebase has poor UI and UX. It is a rough prototype, not the approved product experience.

Developers and coding agents may redesign the information architecture, navigation, layouts, visual language, components, interaction flows, and frontend structure. Do not preserve the current UI merely because it exists. Do not undo another collaborator's design work to conform to the old prototype, screenshots, exported HTML, or earlier Markdown descriptions.

Earlier statements that a dashboard or tab arrangement was confirmed are superseded by this clarification. Product and competitor-research proposals are not a locked implementation checklist. Record changes to product scope separately from choices about how the interface works.

Preserve the meaning of procurement data when redesigning: compare compatible prices and terms, show sources and uncertainty, distinguish potential/agreed/realized savings, and keep internal negotiation limits out of supplier-facing material. These principles do not prescribe screens or components.
