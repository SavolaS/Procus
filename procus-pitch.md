# Procus — pitch outline

Founder-provided pitch, recorded on 19 September 2026. Describes the product vision; demo figures are illustrative.

## 1. Title

**Procus**  
Make every product negotiable.

## 2. Customer example

Industrial equipment manufacturers — e.g. conveyor systems, HVAC units, elevators, packaging machines.

They buy thousands of standardized components: motors, bearings, sensors, cables, fasteners, valves, sheet-metal parts and electronics.

## 3. Problem

Procurement has a long-tail problem.

Teams focus on strategic suppliers, while thousands of smaller SKUs are renewed, reordered and repriced with limited challenge.

## 4. Why now

Raw material prices, supplier pricing and alternatives change constantly.

But procurement teams do not have time to re-check every item, every supplier, every month.

## 5. Current solution

Companies fight this with people-heavy work: procurement teams + consultants.

Illustrative large players: Accenture, Deloitte, PwC, EY and KPMG. Their combined global revenue is roughly $290B+, though not all of this is procurement consulting.

## 6. Core insight

The issue is not that humans are bad at negotiation.

The issue is that humans only have capacity to negotiate the visible head of spend.

## 7. Solution

Procus is an AI procurement co-pilot that makes every product negotiable.

It continuously monitors the product portfolio, detects pricing gaps and prepares negotiation-ready actions.

## 8. Product

Procus tracks:

- Purchase prices by SKU and supplier
- Raw material indices
- Internal comparable products
- External supplier alternatives
- Email/RFQ price signals
- Supplier dependency and buyer negotiation power

## 9. Workflow

Procus detects price drift → recommends negotiation → creates negotiation framework → suggests opening ask and LAA → if needed, launches RFQ agent to find alternative suppliers.

LAA means least acceptable agreement and remains internal to the buyer's negotiation preparation.

## 10. Human + AI position

Procus does not replace procurement relationships.

It gives buyers enough intelligence and preparation to negotiate far more products while keeping the human relationship where it belongs.

## 11. Demo slide

Show one SKU:

- Price up 12%
- Aluminium index up only 3%
- Internal comparable −8%
- Alternative supplier −11%

→ Recommended opening ask: −10%, LAA: −6%.

## 12. Closing

**Procus**  
From tail spend to negotiated spend.
